# Privacy Policy — Unchosen Guard

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how *Unchosen Guard* ("the Game"), developed and published by Tamga Studios ("we," "us," or "our"), handles user information.

Unchosen Guard is a single-player virtual reality game designed for Meta Quest devices. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and processing of personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

Unchosen Guard does not intentionally collect, request, or transmit personal information to us through the Game.

We do not operate our own user accounts, online multiplayer services, advertising systems, or analytics services within the Game.

## 3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The Game may save gameplay progress, settings, and other game-related information locally on your device to support gameplay and allow you to continue your progress.

This locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to us by the Game.

Deleting the Game or its saved data may result in the loss of locally stored progress.

## 4. Meta Quest Platform

The Game runs on the Meta Quest platform. Meta may independently collect and process certain information in accordance with its own privacy policies, platform settings, and applicable terms.

We do not control Meta's independent data collection or processing practices. For more information, please review Meta's Privacy Policy:

https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

## 5. Third-Party Services

We do not intentionally integrate third-party advertising or analytics services into the Game.

If third-party services or software components are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game.

## 7. Data Security and Retention

Because we do not operate our own online accounts or backend services for the Game, we do not maintain a separate online database of player profiles or gameplay progress.

Game data stored locally on your device is subject to the storage, security, and deletion controls available on that device and platform.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

Since we do not intentionally collect personal information through the Game, we generally have no player information of our own to provide, correct, or delete. This does not affect any rights you may have regarding information processed independently by Meta.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published at the same public URL. The "Last Updated" date above will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
